# Supreme Court: Doctors Cannot Be Held Liable for Every Unfortunate Death
In a significant ruling that brings immense relief to the medical fraternity, the Supreme Court has once again reaffirmed: **A doctor is not a guarantor of life, and every unfortunate patient death does not amount to medical negligence.**
### The Supreme Court's Observation
1. **No Guarantee of Cure:** The court reiterated that medicine is an inexact science.
2. **Difference Between Complication and Negligence:** The bench clearly distinguished between an "unfortunate clinical complication" and "culpable negligence."
3. **The Bolam Test Reaffirmed:** Relying on the classic Bolam principle.
**The takeaway is clear:** The Supreme Court will protect you, but only if your documentation protects you first.
