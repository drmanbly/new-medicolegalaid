# Supreme Court Clarifies Burden of Proof in Complex Surgical Complication Cases
In a crucial judicial pronouncement, the Supreme Court of India reaffirmed the fundamental legal distinction between an unavoidable surgical complication and actionable medical negligence.
### 1. Rejection of Automatic *Res Ipsa Loquitur*
The Supreme Court held that the legal doctrine of *res ipsa loquitur* (the thing speaks for itself) cannot be casually applied to complex surgeries. 
### 2. Standard of the 'Ordinary Competent Practitioner'
Revisiting the landmark *Jacob Mathew* standard, the bench reiterated that doctors are not guarantors of miracles. 
### Key Takeaways for Operating Surgeons
1. **Detailed Intra-Operative Notes:** Dictate comprehensive operative notes immediately following surgery.
2. **Informed Consent Specificity:** Ensure that known post-operative complications are explicitly listed.
