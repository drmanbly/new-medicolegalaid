# NCDRC Ruling: Corporate Hospitals Cannot Evade Liability by Labeling Doctors as Independent Contractors
In a significant order impacting hospital administration and private practitioners, the National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission (NCDRC) has ruled that a hospital remains vicariously liable for medical negligence occurring within its premises, even if the treating doctor is classified as a "Visiting Consultant" or "Independent Contractor."
## Strategic Action Plan for Hospital Administrators
To manage institutional risk under the Consumer Protection Act, hospital management should immediately implement the following:
* **Mandatory Professional Indemnity Verification:** Require every visiting consultant to submit proof of active professional indemnity insurance.
* **Memorandum of Understanding (MoU):** Execute formal written agreements with independent practitioners clearly delineating clinical responsibilities.
* **Transparent Patient Disclosures:** Clearly indicate the attending consultant's status in admission booklets.
